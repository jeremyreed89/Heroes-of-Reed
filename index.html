Sir Reed's Sherlock squad meets Vaela's Lestrade squad.

  • Click a shaded hex to move. Click the enemy to attack — the side of the enemy you point at picks where you attack from.

  • Your hero may cast one spell per round from the Spellbook, without using the creature's turn.
